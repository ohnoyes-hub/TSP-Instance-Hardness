Start Core Quadrant

With the core quadrant, you can determine your core qualities, pitfalls, allergies and challenges. Interesting to know is that these are all connected. Your core quality could be directly opposite to your allergy. Also an overkill of your core quality can transgress into a pitfall. The positive opposite of the pitfall is your challenge, which is a good supplement to the core quality. An overkill of your challenge is your allergy.

You can see it as a circle! We would like you to fill in this quadrant below. Don’t worry if you find it too difficult to connect them all. We can help you during the Career Coaching.

Start Questionnaire

This questionnaire is based on the DISC method. This method gives insights into your preferred behavior. Good to know, there are no wrong answers. Choose the statement that suits you best.

As these statements give insights about your behavior at work, please picture yourself being in a work environment when you answer them.
